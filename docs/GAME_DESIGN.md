# Game design

The campaign alternates between management and action. Its decision rhythm is inspired by modern motorsport-management games: a finite cash balance, sponsor retainers, win bonuses, race prizes, performance-gated sponsor tiers, and competing development priorities. Hull work raises efficiency and maximum speed; sails improve drive; crew improves response; strategy improves tactical performance. Five named races provide a compact season arc.

On the water, headings use 32 discrete angles. Propulsive force is derived from the angular difference between heading and wind direction: sailing directly into the wind is slow, reaching is fast, and broad angles remain viable. The near-orthographic camera is aligned SW–NE with the common daytime south-westerly Gulf breeze, placing the windward end upscreen without perspective scaling. Three selectable course tables provide windward/run, Olympic triangle, and 1992 IACC Z racing. Contact and the compact RRS engine determine penalties and speed loss.

All syndicates and sponsors are invented. Multiplayer uses one locally authoritative yacht and up to three remote PRG32 snapshot yachts. AI fills empty slots so every race retains a four-boat fleet.

Each venue uses a distinctive low-memory skyline: Castel dell'Ovo/Santa Lucia, Vesuvius, Capri/Faraglioni, Sorrento, and the Naples waterfront.
